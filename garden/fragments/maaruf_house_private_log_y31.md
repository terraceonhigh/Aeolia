# Maaruf House, Private Family Log — Year 31 SP

*Private. Not for the commercial archive. For family only.*

---

Kamil has returned from Tethic Station. He will not say what happened. He does not need to. His hands have not stopped shaking since he came through the door.

What I know, from what the back-channel networks are saying and from what Kamil will not say:

The Telanik early warning system reported incoming Directorate weapons. For approximately eleven minutes, the Telanik strategic command believed it was under attack. A duty officer — a woman whose name Kamil knows but will not speak — chose not to authorize retaliation. She was correct. The instruments had malfunctioned.

Eleven minutes.

---

I will record what I want to record, which the commercial log cannot contain.

We are the Maaruf house. We have been intermediaries for four generations. We carried the back-channel message that resolved the Keluu Incident in Year 19 — the message that prevented the first near-miss from becoming the first exchange. We have built our house's position on being useful to both sides, on being trusted by both sides, on being necessary. Our neutrality is our livelihood. It is also, if I am honest about it, our shelter: neither side targets intermediaries whose destruction would remove a communication channel the other side might need.

We have built a commercial position on the reasonable expectation that both civilizations will continue to exist.

For eleven minutes, that expectation was wrong.

---

What I want to say, and cannot say in any document that leaves this house:

The people at the Directorate and the Sovereignty who manage the deterrence — the officers, the analysts, the intelligence professionals — they are serious people. They are competent. They have built protocols. They have protocols for the protocols. After the Keluu Incident, we carried the messages that established the first informal bilateral protocol for distinguishing exercises from attacks. That protocol exists because serious, competent people on both sides recognized what had nearly happened and tried to prevent it from happening again.

Those serious, competent people could not prevent an instrument malfunction.

There is no protocol for an instrument malfunction at 3 AM in a command station where the duty officer has to decide, in the time it takes to boil water, whether to end the world. There is no institutional design that removes the requirement for a single person to be right at exactly the wrong moment. There is only the hope that whoever is on duty at that moment has good judgment and is not too tired to use it.

This is what we are depending on. Not the protocols, not the institutions, not the surveillance infrastructure. We are depending on Vaisa Koremata having good judgment at 3 AM.

We are depending on the next Vaisa Koremata having good judgment at the next 3 AM.

---

Kamil asked me, when his hands had stopped shaking enough to hold a cup, whether we should leave. Move the house to a deeper neutral position, further from both command structures, in one of the genuinely uninhabited archipelagos where no strategic interest would justify a weapon's use.

I told him what I believe: there is no such archipelago. On a world that is 95% water, every island is within the arc of something that can reach it from somewhere. There is no distance that provides safety when the weapons travel at the speed they travel. There is only the judgment of the person who decides not to use them.

He asked me whether that was enough.

I said: it has been enough so far.

He said: so far is not a strategy.

He is correct. So far is not a strategy. It is what we have.

---

I am writing this in the private log because the commercial archive cannot hold it. The commercial archive records our position, our fees, our negotiations, our value as intermediaries. It does not record the fact that our value as intermediaries depends entirely on a condition we cannot influence — that two civilizations will continue to choose not to destroy each other, one decision at a time, forever.

The Reach calls this maintenance. The Sovereignty calls it work. The Maaruf house calls it what it is: we are alive today because a woman we have never met decided the instruments were probably wrong.

We will send flowers to the Koremata family. Anonymously, through the Tethic relay. If the back-channel work has taught us anything, it is that the people who prevent catastrophe should know, afterward, that someone noticed.

---

*[Archivist's note, Year 150 SP: The Maaruf house continued to operate as back-channel intermediaries through the remainder of the transition period and into the Strange Peace's formal institutional era. Kamil Maaruf served as the house's principal intermediary until Year 78 SP. The private family log, from which this entry is drawn, was donated to the Intermediate Belt Historical Archive by the family in Year 142 SP, with the note: "We kept this private because we were frightened. We release it now because the people who come after us should know what frightened looked like, so they recognize it if they see it again."]*
