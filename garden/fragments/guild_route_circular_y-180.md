# NAVIGATOR GUILD — INTERNAL CIRCULAR
## From: Route Registry, Guild Hall, Sargasso Basin chapter
## To: All certified route-holders, all relay captains operating outer approaches
## Subject: Reclassification of the Vaur Shelf approach — residual knowledge survey

---

Senior Route-Holder **Tesh Ouen**, Deep Run Grade, died on passage in the fourteenth week of the current season. Her vessel was recovered. There were no survivors. The cause of loss has been classified as weather event in the outer Trade Circuit approach, which is consistent with the season.

---

Tesh Ouen held the routings for the following approaches:

- The northern Vaur Shelf approach (outer gap, cross-swell reading, cloud-fix bearing, land-smell threshold, reef passage entry)
- The eastern Vaur Shelf approach (seasonal variant, Trade Circuit south, night entry by star bearing only)
- The Kalan Basin deep run (partial certification; she had run it four times and was preparing a full route assessment for submission to registry)

Her apprentice, Doru Pelec, holds partial knowledge of the northern Vaur Shelf approach. He has completed seven passages alongside Tesh Ouen. He has not run the approach alone, and the guild does not certify a routing held by one person who has never run it without the route-holder present. The northern Vaur Shelf approach is accordingly reclassified as **unsurveyed** until Doru Pelec completes a supervised solo passage with a guild assessor aboard, or until a second route-holder registers independent familiarity with the approach.

The eastern Vaur Shelf approach and the Kalan Basin deep run have no holder. They are reclassified as **lapsed**. Re-survey from first principles will be required.

---

**Residual knowledge survey:**

Any certified route-holder or relay captain who holds residual knowledge of any of the three approaches listed above is asked to register this knowledge with the guild registry before the close of the current season. Residual knowledge includes: counter-swell bearing at the outer approach; cloud-fix bearing; star bearing for night entry; reef passage entry sequence; land-smell character and threshold distance; any seasonal variation from the standard approach.

Residual knowledge does not need to be complete. Partial residual knowledge may be sufficient to reconstruct a certified routing when combined with a supervised survey passage. The registry wishes to know what you know before it is gone.

---

**Advisory to relay captains:**

Relay captains operating routes that transit the Vaur Shelf approaches are advised to hold off the outer approaches until re-survey is complete. The Kalan Basin deep run should be considered closed. The eastern Vaur Shelf approach is closed.

The northern Vaur Shelf approach may be run by captains who hold their own independent familiarity with the counter-swell pattern and reef passage entry, provided they file a pre-passage notification with the registry. Captains who hold this familiarity but have not registered it are asked to do so.

Captains who do not hold independent familiarity and who have been running the northern Vaur Shelf approach on the basis of Tesh Ouen's route certification should not run this approach until re-survey is certified.

---

**A note from the registry:**

The guild registry has in the past two decades received repeated requests — from commercial operators, from the administered trade network, from the Admiralty — to document the routings in its registry in a form that does not depend on individual route-holders. The registry has declined these requests on the grounds that the documentation cannot capture what the routing contains.

The counter-swell at the outer Vaur Shelf approach runs at approximately 1.5 feet, primary bearing west-southwest of the charted position, secondary bearing northwest. This is a fact that can be written down. What cannot be written down is the ability to feel a 1.5-foot counter-swell beneath a nine-foot primary swell while lying in the hull of a moving vessel in Trade Circuit conditions, to distinguish it from the noise in the water, and to act on it. That ability lives in the route-holder. It is transmitted by hull-lying alongside a senior route-holder on repeated passages. It cannot be transmitted by reading a document.

We say this not to resist improvement but to describe what is actually at stake when a route-holder dies without a certified apprentice. The northern Vaur Shelf approach is closed not because we are withholding information. It is closed because the information that matters cannot be held in any form that survives its holder.

Tesh Ouen ran the northern Vaur Shelf approach for thirty-one years. She ran it in full Trade Circuit weather and in calm. She ran it once in covered sky at night, which she described afterward as the worst decision she had ever made and which produced the most complete description in her personal record. She was the approach. She is gone.

We are asking whether anyone else is her.
